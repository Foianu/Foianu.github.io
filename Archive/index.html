#Texture Mining

Texture Mining began with a simple act: looking closer. Surfaces met in everyday life — some found by chance, others searched for — were explored, photographed and then edited until their hidden patterns came forward.

Neither purely documentary nor fully abstract, the images form a study of how much can live inside a single surface.
